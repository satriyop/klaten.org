# Superadmin is a Club hat; there is no platform role

The operator creates Clubs out of band (seed/SQL, DNS, Caddy) and takes `superadmin` on each Club they provision. Working on Apta means opening `apta.klaten.org`. A parallel platform-admin identity was rejected: it becomes a cross-tenant god UI. Sealed tenants with SSH-only break-glass were rejected: the first locked-out ketua puts you in the database by hand.
